# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Purpose & Standards
We are dedicated to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and professional environment for everyone contributing to this placement intelligence platform.

### Our Expectations
- Communicate with respect, clarity, and constructive feedback.
- Respect diverse viewpoints, professional backgrounds, and skill levels.
- Focus on what is best for the project, its users, and the educational community.
- Handle disagreements professionally and courteously.

### Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, derogatory comments, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without explicit verifiable consent.
- Disruptive or unprofessional conduct during collaboration.

## Enforcement
Maintainers review and address any behavior that contradicts these community guidelines.
